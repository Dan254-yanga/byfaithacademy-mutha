About By Faith Academy – Mutha

By Faith Academy – Mutha is a Christian-based institution located in Mutha, Kenya. We are committed to providing a holistic education that nurtures the spiritual, intellectual, and social growth of every learner. Our mission is to raise God-fearing, responsible, and confident young men and women ready to impact the world.
